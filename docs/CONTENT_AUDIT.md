### Discrepancies and notes
- **Language (important).** The explanations are in **Hindi (Devanagari)**. Business terms are in English, and prompts and scripts are in English or Roman Hinglish. The cover says "A practical business handbook in Hindi and English". The site states this in the hero and in the FAQ, so international and non-Hindi buyers aren't misled.
- **Prompt counts.** The cover says 141. "What you will achieve" says "140+". Bonus A is titled "100+ AI Prompt Library", and the resources page lists "AI prompt library, 104 prompts". These all fit together: the 104-prompt library file plus 37 prompts inside the chapters makes 141.
- **Diagrams.** The cover shows only four stats; it doesn't mention 24 diagrams. The count is verified from the figure captions.
- **Guarantees.** The book explicitly makes no promise of clients or income (Disclaimer, p. iv; Ch. 10). The site follows the same rule.
- **Examples.** Every person and business in the book is hypothetical. Examples include Aman, Farah, Dev, Neha and "Sunrise Dental".

## What makes it different (taken from the book)
- Each chapter's output feeds the next one, with exercises, a checklist and an assignment at the end of every chapter.
- "Workflow cards" lay out each task as What / Why / How / Tool / Tool use / Output / Verify / Mistakes.
- Every prompt has a PURPOSE / OUTPUT / VERIFY line.
- Every chapter has an Indian market application and international context.
- It is honest about its limits. Examples are labelled hypothetical, prices are dated, and there is a list of what couldn't be verified.
